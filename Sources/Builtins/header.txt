
    @header <name>, [parameters ...]
    @header(expose) <name>, [parameters ...]

Defines `<name>` as a symbol to reuse the block of instructions and/or data,
including labels when the header is unrolled with a namespace label.

The `expose` option makes this macro public for `@import`.



snippets/builtin-header.s:
    @header mutex_ulock, addr
                      xch zr, addr
    @end

    @section root

    _:                mutex_ulock a1


    @err <message>, [formatting expressions ...]

Generates an assemble error when evaluated by the semantic analyser.

This may be used in conjunction with `@if` for assemble-time validation, or to
signal deprecation errors when an old block or `@header` is referenced.



snippets/builtin-err.s:
    @header Queue, len
    @align 2

    @if len < 1
          @err "Queue len may not be zero or negative; found %d", len
    @end

    head:             u16 0
    tail:             u16 0
    memory:           reserve u8, len
    @end

    @section root

    _:                Queue 0

$ qcpu snippets/builtin-err.s
    snippets/builtin-err.s:6:7: error: Queue len may not be zero or negative; found 0
          @err "Queue len may not be zero or negative; found %d", len
          ^~~~
    snippets/builtin-err.s:5:9: note: conditionally evaluated from here
    @if len < 1
            ^
    snippets/builtin-err.s:16:19: note: Queue unrolled from here
    _:                Queue 0
                      ^~~~~



The following formatting arguments are available:

    %d - discrete integer
    %b - binary
    %h - hexadecimal
    %s - string
    %a - any, internal representation of argument

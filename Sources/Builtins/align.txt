
    @align <alignment>

Emits some amount of padding bytes to conform to the set alignment during
linkage. `<alignment>` must be positive, non-zero, and a power of two.



snippets/builtin-align.s:
    @section example

    _:                u8 0xEA
    @align 2
    _:                u16 0xDEAD

$ qcpu --dlinker --noelimination snippets/builtin-align.s
    ... (omitted)
    @section example (5 bytes)
        0x0003   * 1 byte
        0x0004   0xEA (234)
        0x0005   * 1 byte
        0x0006   0xDEAD (57005)

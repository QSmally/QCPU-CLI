
    @import <name>, <file path>
    @import(expose) <name>, <file path>

Imports the public symbols of another file into a namespace.

The `expose` option makes this imported namespace public for `@import`.



snippets/builtin-import.s:
    @import namespace, "builtin-define.s"

    @section root

    _:                u16 namespace.foo

        0x0000   0xBEEF (48879)

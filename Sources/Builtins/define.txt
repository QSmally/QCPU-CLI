
    @define <name>, <expression>
    @define(expose) <name>, <expression>

Defines `<name>` as a symbol to reuse `<expression>`.

The `expose` option makes this macro public for `@import`.



snippets/builtin-define.s:
    @define(expose) foo, 0xBEEF

    @section root

    _:                lui d1, foo'u

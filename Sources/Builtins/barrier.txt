
    @barrier
    @barrier(noelimination)

Splits the current section into another block, to mark non-continuous execution
flow, which allows dead-code elimination to further optimise the output. Alias
of `@section <same section name>`.

The `noelimination` option ensures the block is not optimised away during
semantic analysis.



snippets/builtin-barrier.s:
    @section a

    .foo:             bkpt ; referenced

    @section b

    .bar:             bkpt ; not referenced

$ qcpu --dlinker snippets/builtin-barrier.s
    ... (omitted)
